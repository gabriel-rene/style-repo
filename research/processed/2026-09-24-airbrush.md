# Airbrush

## summary
An airbrush is a small handheld tool attached to a supply of compressed air that sprays paint in a controlled manner, and airbrushing is the painting technique that uses it to produce smooth, even surfaces, often in pursuit of strong realism. The instrument was invented in the late nineteenth century but was taken up widely by artists only in the mid-twentieth century. Illustrators of the 1930s and 1940s used it, as did later Pop and science fiction artists, and digital airbrush software appeared in the early 1980s.
