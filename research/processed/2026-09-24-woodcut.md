# Woodcut

## summary
Woodcut is a relief printing technique in which a wooden block is carved so that the image stands up from the surface. The raised areas are inked and paper is pressed onto them, either in a press or by applying pressure by hand, as with Japanese ukiyo-e prints. In Europe, woodblock printing appeared in the twelfth century for decorating textiles, and its use for printing on paper followed by the late fourteenth century.
