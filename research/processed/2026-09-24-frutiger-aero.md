# Frutiger Aero

## summary
Frutiger Aero is a name catalogued by the Consumer Aesthetics Research Institute for a corporate technology aesthetic of roughly the mid-2000s to the early 2010s. The label joins Windows Vista's Aero design guidelines with Adrian Frutiger's typeface Frutiger. Its typical features include skeuomorphic interfaces, glossy finishes, glassy or transparent materials, humanist sans-serif type and photographic motifs such as auroras, bokeh and close-up grass. It is also known as Aero, Aqua or Web 2.0.
