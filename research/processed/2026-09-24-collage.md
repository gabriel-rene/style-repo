# Collage

## summary
Collage names both a technique and the artwork it produces, in which paper, photographs, fabric and other ephemera are arranged and glued onto a support. The word comes from the French papiers collés, or découpage, which referred to pasting cut paper onto surfaces. Artists first adopted it as a technique in the early twentieth century. A collage may also combine painting and drawing and can include three-dimensional elements.
