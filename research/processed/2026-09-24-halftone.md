# Halftone

## summary
Halftone is a method for reproducing continuous-tone artwork on printing presses that can apply only a single solid colour of ink per plate. When the image was photographed onto film, a fine screen of perpendicular intersecting lines was set between the film and the original, breaking the picture into thousands of tiny dots. The dots are larger and closer together in dark areas and smaller and farther apart in light ones, so the eye perceives smooth gradations of tone rather than individual dots.
