# Cybercore

## summary
Cybercore is an aesthetic, described by NYLON primarily as a fashion trend, that is strongly shaped by the early days of the internet and sits alongside the broader Y2K revival. It combines futuristic, science fiction influences with nostalgia, mixing techwear, cyberpunk and 2000s styles. Its visual references include the look of Photoshop and digital editing and old web material such as GIFs, video games and clip art, along with bright colors, rhinestones and metallics. The article notes wide circulation of the style on TikTok.
