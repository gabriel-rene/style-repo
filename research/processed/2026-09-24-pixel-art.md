# Pixel art

## summary
Pixel art is digital art made in graphics software with the individual pixel as its only building unit. It is closely tied to the low-resolution imagery of 8-bit and 16-bit era computers, arcade machines and game consoles, and works typically limit colour palettes and file size, whether from technical constraint or for aesthetic effect. The term was first published in 1982 by Adele Goldberg and Robert Flegal at Xerox PARC, and the form saw renewed popularity in the 2010s through games such as Undertale and Stardew Valley.
