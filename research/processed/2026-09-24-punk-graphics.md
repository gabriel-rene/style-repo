# Punk graphics

## summary
Punk graphics are the flyers, posters, album covers, promotional material, zines and related ephemera produced around punk and post-punk music, surveyed in an exhibition covering 1976 to 1986 that included material from New York's CBGB scene. Designers relied on strategies such as parody, pastiche, appropriation and collage, and drew on science fiction, horror and comics. Over the decade the look shifted from spare black-and-white minimalism toward the broader colour and expressive forms of New Wave graphics.
