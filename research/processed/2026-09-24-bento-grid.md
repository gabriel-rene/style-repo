# Bento grid

## summary
A bento grid is a user interface layout pattern modeled on the compartments of a traditional Japanese bento box, dividing content into distinct, clearly organized sections. A 2024 Muzli article describes it as a design trend that has become a fixture of UI and UX work, pairing a well-defined visual hierarchy with a minimalist, functional look. The article notes the approach is not entirely new, and its examples include work from companies such as Apple and GitHub.
