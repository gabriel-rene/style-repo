# Linocut

## summary
A linocut is a relief print made much like a woodcut, except that the design is cut into and printed from linoleum. The block is a thin layer of linoleum, a canvas backing coated with solidified linseed oil, generally mounted on wood. Because linoleum is soft and has no grain, it can be cut in any direction more easily than wood, leaving a raised surface whose slight texture takes ink evenly. Linoleum was invented as flooring in the nineteenth century and became popular for printmaking among artists and amateurs in the twentieth.
