# Pen plotter

## summary
A pen plotter is a drawing machine that makes marks with pen and ink on paper, directed either directly or through code. Architects and engineers used pen plotters from the early computer era of the 1960s and 1970s to produce construction drawings, but from the 1990s they were largely replaced by faster inkjet printers. The machines have since been revived by artists, who value the blend of digital accuracy with the analogue relationship of pen, ink and paper, and who use them to generate algorithmic marks, sometimes on custom-built flatbed or hanging plotters.
