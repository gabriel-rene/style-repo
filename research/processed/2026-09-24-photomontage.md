# Photomontage

## summary
Photomontage is a form of collage built from photographs, often used as a vehicle for political protest. Dadaists took it up in 1915 as part of their opposition to the First World War, and the surrealists later used it to combine unrelated images in ways suggestive of the unconscious. From 1923 the Russian constructivist Aleksander Rodchenko applied it to socially engaged work, while John Heartfield reworked media imagery against German Fascism and Peter Kennard addressed inequality and nuclear arms from the 1970s to the 1990s.
