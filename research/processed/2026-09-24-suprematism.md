# Suprematism

## summary
Suprematism is an abstract art movement that Kazimir Malevich named and developed from 1913. Its paintings are built from simple geometric shapes such as squares, circles, lines and rectangles in a restricted palette, usually set floating on a white ground, with colour and spatial relations central to the effect. The first suprematist exhibition was held in St Petersburg in December 1915, and artists including El Lissitzky and László Moholy-Nagy later took up its approach.
