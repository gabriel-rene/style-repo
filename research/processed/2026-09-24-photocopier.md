# Photocopier

## summary
The photocopier became a creative and activist tool from the 1960s onward, used by artists and by communities producing independent music presses, activist papers and feminist publications in the 1970s and 1980s. Copier-based work surveyed in a Bay Area exhibition includes fanzines, postcards, mailers and collage, sometimes hand-coloured after copying. Artists used it, for example, to assemble collage booklets mixing traditional Mexican imagery with American comic characters, circulating tactile printed matter in the era before the internet.
