# Neumorphism

## summary
Neumorphism is a user interface trend that blends skeuomorphism with minimalism to produce soft, almost three-dimensional surfaces. Its name is a contraction of new skeuomorphism, and it is also called soft UI because of its low contrast. According to the Interaction Design Foundation, Michał Malewicz of Hype4 coined the term in 2019 after noticing such designs on Dribbble and Behance, with a viral banking app by Alexander Plyuto often cited as the first example. Typical traits include subtle light and dark shadows, muted semi-flat colors and rounded corners.
