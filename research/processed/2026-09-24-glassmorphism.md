# Glassmorphism

## summary
Glassmorphism is a visual design style that imitates frosted glass, using varying degrees of translucency to separate foreground elements from the background and suggest depth. According to Nielsen Norman Group, it has been gaining popularity since 2020 and features prominently in the design systems of Apple and Microsoft. Its main ingredients are reduced opacity and background blur, often refined with strokes and gradients. Used carefully it can build hierarchy, but overuse can create accessibility and usability problems.
