# Chromolithography

## summary
Chromolithography is a colour printing process that marked a turning point in the history of product packaging, which before it was mostly printed in one colour or coloured by hand. It built on lithography, a monochrome method discovered by Alois Senefelder in 1798 and brought to Britain in 1801. Godefroy Engelmann patented chromolithography in 1837, and it reached Britain from the 1840s. The Museum of Brands describes it as the printmaking process that brought colour to a mass audience.
