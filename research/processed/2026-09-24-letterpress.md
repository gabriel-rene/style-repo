# Letterpress

## summary
Letterpress is a printing process in which raised surfaces, typically of wood, metal or linoleum, are inked and pressed onto paper. It is essentially the relief printing method invented by Johannes Gutenberg in the fifteenth century, using movable type, hand-carved blocks and hand-cranked presses. Carved blocks and movable letters are set in reverse, locked into a frame called a chase, inked and printed so the impression reads correctly. Each additional colour requires its own assembled form and a separate pass through the press.
