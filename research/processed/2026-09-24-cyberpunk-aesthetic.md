# Cyberpunk aesthetic

## summary
Cyberpunk is a science fiction subgenre set in dystopian futures that pairs advanced technology such as artificial intelligence and cyberware with societal collapse and decay, a combination summed up as lowlife and high tech. The word first appeared as the title of a Bruce Bethke short story written in 1980 and published in 1983, and William Gibson's 1984 novel Neuromancer helped solidify the genre. Its settings are usually post-industrial dystopias with an atmosphere echoing film noir, and early screen examples include Ridley Scott's 1982 film Blade Runner.
