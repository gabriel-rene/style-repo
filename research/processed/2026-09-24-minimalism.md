# Minimalism

## summary
Minimalism in graphic design is a pursuit of simplification marked by flat colours, clean type, often geometric sans-serif wordmarks, and generous whitespace. The article traces this impulse to Adolf Loos's 1910 attack on ornament, the Bauhaus and its motto less is more, and the International Typographic Style, whose grids and few typefaces spread into corporate identity. It notes that these earlier designers did not call their work minimalist, and describes a recent wave of startup minimalism in Silicon Valley rebrands such as Google and Uber, alongside the move from skeuomorphic to flat interfaces.
