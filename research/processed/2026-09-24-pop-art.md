# Pop art

## summary
Pop art is an art movement that arose in Britain in the mid 1950s and in America in the late 1950s, reaching its height in the 1960s. Rebelling against prevailing ideas of what art should be, its artists drew imagery from popular and commercial sources such as Hollywood films, advertising, packaging, pop music and comic books. American pop marked a return to representation after abstract expressionism, while British pop took a more academic approach, examining what American popular imagery signified and how it shaped lifestyles.
