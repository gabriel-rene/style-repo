# Claymorphism

## summary
Claymorphism is a user interface design trend that gives elements a soft, inflated three-dimensional look. A 2022 Smashing Magazine article credits the term to Michal Malewicz, who had also been behind the earlier neumorphism trend. The effect is built from two inner shadows, one dark and one light, plus a usually darker outer shadow, which together make elements appear soft and floating. Unlike neumorphism, it can be used on any background color, easing that style's problems with visual hierarchy and contrast.
