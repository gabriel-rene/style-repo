# Grunge typography

## summary
Grunge typography is an experimental lettering style that peaked in the 1990s, in step with the popularity of the Macintosh computer. It is marked by chaotic, splayed words, blurred and overlapping letters and heavy textures, abandoning conventional kerning, leading and spacing so that letters become art, often at the expense of readability. Designer Carlos Segura compared it to punk as a backlash against clean, classical design, and David Carson, linked with Ray Gun magazine, is described as its godfather. By the mid-2000s demand had shifted toward clean lettering.
