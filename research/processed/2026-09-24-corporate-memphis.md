# Corporate Memphis

## summary
Corporate Memphis is a label for a flat, colourful, minimal illustration style showing energetic figures with long legs, lanky arms and small torsos, used heavily by large technology companies and start-ups. Commentators commonly trace it to Alegria, an illustration system made by the agency Buck and most famously used by Facebook. The article argues that the label is disputed. Some illustrators prefer the neutral term flat art, and collections claiming to show the style disagree widely about what it includes.
