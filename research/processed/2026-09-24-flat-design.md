# Flat design

## summary
Flat design is a web and interface style defined by the absence of glossy or three-dimensional visual effects, avoiding shadows, gradients, highlights and realistic textures. It gained prominence around 2011 and 2012, strongly shaped by Microsoft's Metro design language and the release of Windows 8, which Microsoft presented as exploring the digital medium rather than imitating the physical world. Apple's adoption around 2013 further confirmed the trend, which arose as a reaction against overused 3D effects and skeuomorphism.
