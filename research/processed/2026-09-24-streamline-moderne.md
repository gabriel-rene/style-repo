# Streamline Moderne

## summary
Streamline Moderne is an international strand of Art Deco architecture and design that appeared in the 1930s. Drawing on aerodynamic design, it favoured curving forms, long horizontal lines and sometimes nautical motifs, and was applied in industrial design to suggest sleekness and modernity; in France it was called the ocean liner style, after the SS Normandie launched in 1932. Buildings often featured rounded corners, horizontal wall grooves, glass brick, porthole windows, flat roofs and chrome hardware, commonly finished in white or muted pastels.
