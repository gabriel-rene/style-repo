# Superflat

## summary
Superflat is a term devised by the artist Murakami Takashi to name both a pull toward flat, planar imagery in Japanese art and a perceived superficiality in the culture around it. It refers to an art movement that began in Japan in the mid-1990s and to a critical lens on postwar Japanese society, developed through exhibitions such as Superflat in 2000. Visually it favours flatness and lavish decoration, borrows from anime and manga, and blurs the line between fine art and popular or commercial culture.
