# Scrapbook

## summary
A scrapbook is a personal assemblage of found visual material, such as cutouts and glued-in artifacts, that records an individual life in a way comparable to a diary. Writing in Print magazine in 2016, Steven Heller draws on Jessica Helfand's 2008 study of American scrapbooks, which framed the form as an early open means of self-expression built on visual sampling, cultural mixing and the reuse of existing media. Meaning in a scrapbook often emerges from the juxtaposition of its pasted-in pieces.
