# Vienna Secession

## summary
The Vienna Secession was formed in 1897 when Gustav Klimt led a group of young Austrian artists and architects out of the Vienna Künstlerhaus, then dominated by academic traditionalists. The group aimed to transform not just the visual arts but aesthetic life as a whole. In 1898 it launched the journal Ver Sacrum and built its own exhibition hall, which showed its members' work and brought international avant-garde art to Vienna. In this period Klimt's art turned toward a flatter, more linear manner influenced by Greek vase painting and Japanese woodcuts.
