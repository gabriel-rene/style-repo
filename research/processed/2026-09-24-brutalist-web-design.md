# Brutalist web design

## summary
Web brutalism is a contested movement in contemporary web interaction design that pushes back against the standardised visual conventions that dominate the web. A 2017 paper in the journal Dialectic, summarised by the AIGA Design Educators Community, presents it as a debate. One side sees it as a welcome rejection of templates and one-size-fits-all design. The other argues it distracts from pressing accessibility and usability problems. Brutalist sites are described as deliberately intense and coarse.
