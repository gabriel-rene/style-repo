# Surrealism

## summary
Surrealism was a twentieth-century movement spanning art, literature and philosophy. Guillaume Apollinaire coined the word in 1917, and André Breton defined the movement in his 1924 Surrealist Manifesto around the idea of psychic automatism. Surrealists set out to release the unconscious and dreams, using automatic drawing and writing and depicting dreamlike scenes to find strange beauty in the unexpected and uncanny. Figures associated with it include Salvador Dalí, René Magritte, Max Ernst, Joan Miró and Leonora Carrington.
