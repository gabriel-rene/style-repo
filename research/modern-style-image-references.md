# Contemporary Image Reference Shortlist

This is a research shortlist for the repository’s 19 entries typed as **style**. It favors 2024–2026 work and direct project pages over image aggregators. Each link is a visual reference, not an asset license: do not download, reproduce, or add imagery to the library without separately confirming the creator’s usage terms.

**Selection rule.** “Modern take” means a current project that explicitly names, or clearly and thoughtfully reworks, the entry’s visual language. The list is deliberately capped below five items where stronger, attributable examples were more useful than filler. Creator/studio names are the attribution supplied by the linked project page; dates are publication dates when available.

## Art Deco

- [Conceptual Illustration Poster — Art Deco Style](https://www.behance.net/gallery/254507479/Conceptual-Illustration-Poster-Art-Deco-Style) — Apurva Kabra, 2026. A dark, editorial cocktail poster that updates Deco framing and elongated type.
- [Modern Art Deco Poster](https://www.behance.net/gallery/245461369/Modern-Art-Deco-Poster) — Anna Elina S., 2026. A compact study in stylised portraiture, warm tonality, and geometric composition.
- [Art decó inspired POSTER SERIES](https://www.behance.net/gallery/234344283/Art-deco-inspired-POSTER-SERIES) — Ainhoa Santana Rúa, 2025. Cultural-event posters using Deco geometry and narrative illustration.

## Art Nouveau

- [ART NOUVEAU](https://www.behance.net/gallery/237607045/ART-NOUVEAU) — Guillermo Turiel Ferrero, 2025. A deliberately pared-back, platform-ready reinterpretation of Steinlen-inflected poster language.
- [Social Media Graphic: Art Nouveau](https://www.behance.net/gallery/216679621/Social-Media-Graphic-Art-Nouveau) — Rosalie Gomez, 2025. A useful before/after study of botanical framing adapted for social formats.
- [Graphic Design Trends 2025 Poster Series](https://www.behance.net/gallery/216184817/Graphic-Design-Trends-2025-Poster-Series-by-Zeka-Design/modules/1230825437) — Zeka Design / Yaroslav Iakovlev, 2025. A high-density poster reference that explicitly pairs Art Nouveau and maximalist language.

## Brutalist web design

- [Studio Brot](https://www.todaymade.com/blog/brutalist-web-design) — Studio Brot, featured in TodayMade’s 2025 roundup. Strong oversized type and intentionally raw interaction; follow the linked studio site for the live experience.
- [56 Digital](https://elementor.com/blog/brutalism-in-web-design/) — 56 Digital, documented by Elementor. A spare portfolio structure whose hover-revealed project list is a particularly useful interaction reference.
- [Roze Bunker](https://reallygooddesigns.com/neo-brutalist-website-examples/) — Roze Bunker, featured by Really Good Designs, 2025. A contemporary neo-brutalist retail application.
- [Brutalist Websites](https://brutalistwebsites.com/) — curated by Pascal Deville. A living, creator-linked image index; use individual project pages for final attribution.

## Grunge typography

- [Frayed Threadbare Fabric Textured Display Font](https://www.behance.net/gallery/233469779/Frayed-Threadbare-Fabric-Textured-Display-Font) — Michelle Still, 2025. A current display-face study in distressed, fabric-like texture.
- [Grunge Typography Zine](https://www.behance.net/gallery/121326077/Grunge-Typography-Zine) — Kavinu Kankanange, 2021. Older, but still a strong applied reference for scanned marks, restrained colour, and editorial pacing.
- [Shibuya Punk Posters — Visual Series](https://www.behance.net/gallery/237542071/Shibuya-Punk-Posters-Visual-Series) — Rafael Ribeiro de Michelli, 2025. Cross-over reference for photostat noise, halftone, irregular grids, and experimental lettering.

## Ligne claire

- [EUROSTAR — Together We Go Further](https://www.behance.net/gallery/202117961/EUROSTAR-Together-We-Go-Further) — Felipe Portugal, Daniel Lopes, Max Andrade, and Diego Sanchez / The Mill, 2024. The strongest commercial example: a Brussels animation explicitly conceived as a modern homage to ligne claire.
- [Campus Politics Illustration for The New York Times](https://www.behance.net/gallery/209792459/Campus-Politics-Illustration-for-The-New-York-Times) — Allie Sullberg, 2024. Editorial illustration that combines clean linework with contemporary colour and texture.
- [Illustration for a Book cover in a ligne claire style](https://www.behance.net/gallery/214528653/Illustration-for-a-Book-cover-in-a-ligne-claire-style) — Marina Giletich, 2024. A book-cover application with clear author, illustrator, and cover-design attribution.

## Maximalism

- [MUCH — A Visual Exhibition on Maximalism](https://www.behance.net/gallery/241742037/MUCH-A-Visual-Exhibition-on-Maximalism) — Olya Bobyk, 2026. The clearest recent anchor: excess treated as a consistent system across poster and motion work.
- [Human Emotions — Maximalism Poster Collection](https://www.behance.net/gallery/232068569/Human-Emotions-Maximalism-Poster-Collection-%28vol-01%29) — Ulkar Hajizada, 2025. Dense symbolism, distorted imagery, and colour conflict applied to an expressive poster series.
- [Graphic Design Trends 2025 Poster Series](https://www.behance.net/gallery/216184817/Graphic-Design-Trends-2025-Poster-Series-by-Zeka-Design/modules/1230825437) — Zeka Design / Yaroslav Iakovlev, 2025. Useful for comparing maximalism directly against more organic Art Nouveau cues.

## Mid-century modern

- [Arches of Armenia](https://www.behance.net/gallery/237570331/Arches-of-Armenia) — Sona Vardanyan, 2025. Heritage posters built from mid-century geometry, modern palette choices, and balanced type.
- [Website Design — Case Study Houses](https://www.behance.net/gallery/223651403/Website-Design-Case-Study-Houses) — Olivia Rudschewski, 2025. A translation of the style’s architectural logic into a functional web layout.
- [MID CENTURY MODERN WHISKEY BOTTLE DESIGN: BE HUMAN](https://www.behance.net/gallery/222095623/MID-CENTURY-MODERN-WHISKEY-BOTTLE-DESIGN-BE-HUMAN) — Ruth Rohrer, 2025. Packaging/label application rather than poster pastiche.

## Minimalism

- [Kātde®](https://www.behance.net/gallery/225541809/Katde) — Wessam Eltantawy, 2025. Particularly strong for warm minimalism: quiet wordmark, controlled patterns, and considered spacing.
- [30 DESIGN BOLD MINIMALISM](https://www.behance.net/gallery/221074571/30-DESIGN-BOLD-MINIMALISM) — Oky Darmawan, 2025. A broad current visual survey of concise, type-led compositions.
- [Poster Series | Safe Place](https://www.behance.net/gallery/234486807/Poster-Series-Safe-Place) — Anna Zlotina, 2025. Minimalism used for atmosphere and narrative rather than merely reduction.

## New Wave typography

- [New Wave Pizza & Natural Wine Festival](https://www.behance.net/search/projects/new%20wave%20typography) — zsolt zleovszki. A prominent current project surfaced by Behance’s New Wave typography index; open the creator card before reuse to capture its project-specific URL and usage terms.
- [Nouvelle Vague: A Typographic Translation](https://www.behance.net/gallery/236348861/Nouvelle-Vague-A-Typographic-Translation) — Isabella Freeland, 2025. Not a literal New Wave revival, but an excellent contemporary reference for fractured grids, analogue texture, and type as narrative.
- [New-Wave Typography | Tribute to Wolfgang Weingart](https://www.behance.net/gallery/4451169/New-Wave-Typography-Tribute-to-Wolfgang-Weingart) — Irfan Ghani, 2012. Historical but valuable as a clearly credited formal baseline; pair it with current work above.

## Op art

- [Op Art](https://www.behance.net/gallery/159204401/Op-Art) — Max Drekker, 2022. High-contrast black-and-white optical patterns applied in a clean contemporary poster treatment.
- [Typographic Posters](https://www.behance.net/gallery/11594575/Typographic-Posters) — Manolo Guerrero, 2013–2015. Older but award-recognized and unusually rigorous: type, lines, pattern, and isometric perspective create the optical effect.

## Pixel art

- [Pixel art style / Branding in pixel art style](https://www.behance.net/gallery/181135479/Pixel-art-style-Branding-in-pixel-art-style) — Yulia Mityukova, 2023. A focused identity application spanning character, logo, app, and brand touchpoints.
- [Pixel Art](https://www.behance.net/search/projects/pixel%20art) — Behance’s current creator index. Use it as discovery only; attribute any selected image to its project owner and retain the project URL.

## Plakatstil

- [NIKE MODERN “PLAKATSTIL”](https://www.behance.net/gallery/118068151/NIKE-MODERN-PLAKATSTIL) — A2X_ Design, 2021. Direct contemporary product-ad application; especially useful for object isolation and negative space.
- [Plakatstil poster design](https://www.behance.net/gallery/170072391/Plakatstil-poster-design) — Kaden Vela, 2023. A concise, attributable study in simplified commodity poster language.
- [Plakatstil Projects](https://www.behance.net/search/projects/plakatstil?locale=en) — Behance creator index. It currently surfaces named modern exercises including Plakatstil “Poster Style” Candy Bar poster (Maddy Boehme) and Levi’s: A Modern Plakatstil (Varditer Hovhannisyan); open individual projects before archival use.

## Pop art

- [“A.W” exhibition poster design, Pinelo Art Gallery](https://www.behance.net/gallery/226166075/AW-exhibition-poster-design-Pinelo-Art-Gallery) — Beyza Yayla, 2025. A particularly useful bridge between screen-print precedent and contemporary digital-pixel texture.
- [Retro Glamour: The Pop Art Sensation](https://www.behance.net/gallery/221413277/Retro-Glamour-The-Pop-Art-Sensation) — SwatiManish IraAarav, 2025. Contemporary digital precision applied to comic-book glamour.
- [Pop Art// Bold Comic Style Posters](https://www.behance.net/gallery/237255713/Pop-Art-Bold-Comic-Style-Tomato-Poster-) — Romaisa Yasmeen, 2025. Small-scale but crisp example of object-led pop-poster composition.
- [Retro Pop Art Abstract Posters Set](https://www.behance.net/gallery/233733943/Retro-Pop-Art-Abstract-Posters-Set) — Darko Vujic, 2025. Useful for the geometric/portrait branch of current pop revival.

## Psychedelic art

- [Afrodelic Dreamscape — Personal Project](https://www.behance.net/gallery/230866353/Afrodelic-Dreamscape-Personal-Project) — Leandro Massai, 2025. An intentional contemporary fusion of psychedelic art, surrealism, and Afrofuturist imagery.
- [ECHO | Rap Culture Catalyst | Immersive Music Branding](https://www.behance.net/gallery/221972877/ECHO-Rap-Culture-Catalyst-Immersive-Music-Branding) — Mathis Olivencia, 2025. Adjacent rather than literal psychedelia, but strong for fluid forms, kinetic type, tactile materials, and immersive application.

## Punk graphics

- [Shibuya Punk Posters — Visual Series](https://www.behance.net/gallery/237542071/Shibuya-Punk-Posters-Visual-Series) — Rafael Ribeiro de Michelli, 2025. Most useful recent graphic reference: monochrome photos, one accent colour, halftone, irregular grids, and Latin/Katakana collage.
- [The Punk Look Series](https://www.behance.net/gallery/238295563/The-Punk-Look-Series) — Leslie Daniell, 2025. Book-jacket application of punk graphic language.
- [Punk](https://www.behance.net/gallery/220108337/Punk) — David Petznick, 2025. Character-led punk reference with motion variants.

## Streamline Moderne

- [Stantec EVO](https://www.behance.net/gallery/182074753/Stantec-EVO) — AIA Tampa Bay / Stantec, 2023. The strongest recent, directly attributable example found: a completed St. Petersburg residential development explicitly inspired by Streamline Moderne and Florida modernism.
- [The Normandie Hotel, San Juan](https://www.normandiehotelpr.com/) — Félix Benítez Rexach, original architect; current hotel operator. Not a new build, but a locally relevant architectural image reference for liner silhouette, portholes, and horizontal banding. Confirm current photography rights with the hotel before use.

## Superflat

- [Ikebana Murakami](https://www.behance.net/gallery/112042015/Ikebana-Murakami) — Jon Buckley, ongoing series, published 2021. A contemporary still-life recontextualization of Murakami’s Superflat flowers within ikebana.
- [Takashi Murakami, “Flowers”](https://gagosian.com/exhibitions/2025/takashi-murakami-japan-art-festival-hanoi/) — Takashi Murakami / Gagosian, 2025 exhibition context. Primary-artist reference for a current presentation; check the gallery’s image rights before any reproduction.

## Swiss International Style

- [50 Swiss Style Posters](https://www.behance.net/gallery/221014645/50-Swiss-Style-Posters) — Anton Semjonov, 2025. The richest current volume reference for type, rhythm, and poster variation.
- [Swiss style posters](https://www.behance.net/gallery/216983667/Swiss-style-posters) — Maria Holovachova, 2025. Explicit grid and golden-ratio construction notes make it useful for analysis.
- [Swiss Style Movie Poster](https://www.behance.net/gallery/225035539/Swiss-Style-Movie-Poster) — Nessie S, 2025. A clear adaptation to contemporary entertainment-key-art format.
- [Swiss Style Typography Poster Design](https://www.behance.net/gallery/236571647/Swiss-Style-Typography-Poster-Design) — Nayem Ur Rahman Shishir, 2025. Strong grid, hierarchy, negative space, and sans-serif type study.

## Ukiyo-e

- [Yokai in the City — Ukiyo-e Billboards](https://www.behance.net/gallery/244155221/Yokai-in-the-City-Ukiyo-e-Billboards) — shinjiro kimura, 2026. The standout contemporary revival: reconstructed vectors placed in current urban advertising contexts.
- [Ukiyo-e Packaging Prototype](https://www.behance.net/gallery/241837413/Ukiyo-e-Packaging-Prototype) — shinjiro kimura, 2026. A restrained packaging-system experiment rather than a decorative quotation.
- [Japanese-Inspired Fashion Illustrations](https://www.behance.net/gallery/233552069/Japanese-Inspired-Fashion-Illustrations) — Anastasia Tkachuk, 2025. A clearly credited blend of ukiyo-e motifs and current anime-fashion reference points.

## Curation notes

- Do not mark any of these references as approved repository imagery merely because they are linked here. The library’s image policy still requires source URL, attribution, rights status, and human relevance review.
- Behance links generally identify a creator but do **not** grant reuse rights. Treat each as `restricted` or `unknown` until its licensing terms are verified.
- The two creator-index links are discovery leads, deliberately called out as such; they are not sufficient provenance for an individual image record.
